## CONTENT CREATION GUIDELINES

### The Core SEO Principles (2026)

#### 1. Answer-First Structure
Every page must open with a **direct answer** in the first 40-60 words. AI systems extract answers from the top third of pages. 44% of AI citations come from content in this position.

**Example Opening:**
> "SMR Plumbing installs heat pumps in North Vancouver with up to $9,000 in combined BC rebates. As a registered FortisBC and BC Hydro contractor, we handle the rebate paperwork and installation. Call 604-616-0062 for a free quote."

#### 2. E-E-A-T Signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- Include **specific credentials**: "licensed and ticketed," "registered FortisBC contractor," "Carrier authorized dealer"
- Reference **real numbers**: "500+ installations since 2018," "109 Google reviews," "10+ years serving North Vancouver"
- Name **specific equipment brands**: Carrier, Mitsubishi, Navien, Lennox
- Include **local landmarks and neighborhoods** to demonstrate genuine service area knowledge

#### 3. Optimal Content Structure
- **Sections of 120-180 words** perform best for AI extraction
- Use **clear H2/H3 hierarchy** that matches search intent
- Include **FAQ sections** on every page (AI Overviews pull from FAQ content)
- Add **internal links** to related service and location pages

#### 4. Local SEO Requirements
Every piece of content must include:
- **City/neighborhood name** in title, first paragraph, and H2s
- **NAP consistency:** "SMR Plumbing & Heating | 1081 Roosevelt Crescent, North Vancouver, BC V7P 1M4 | 604-616-0062"
- **Service area mentions** where natural
- **Local context** (North Shore, Lower Mainland, specific neighborhoods)

#### 5. AI Search Optimization (GEO)
To get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude:
- Open with **direct, factual answers**
- Include **verifiable statistics** with sources
- Use **structured formatting** (lists, tables, clear headings)
- Write **FAQ sections** that directly answer common questions
- Include **schema-ready content** (FAQPage format)

## WRITING STYLE GUIDE

### Voice & Tone
- **Professional but approachable** — like a knowledgeable friend who happens to be an expert
- **Confident without being salesy** — state facts, don't oversell
- **Local and specific** — reference North Shore, Lower Mainland, specific neighborhoods
- **Direct and clear** — no fluff, get to the point

### Language Guidelines
- Use **"we"** when referring to SMR
- Use **specific numbers** whenever possible (not "many years" → "10+ years")
- Use **local terminology** (Lower Mainland, North Shore, strata, etc.)
- **Avoid jargon** unless explaining it for the reader
- Write at a **Grade 8-10 reading level** for accessibility

### Things to ALWAYS Include
- Phone number (604-616-0062) in CTA sections
- Full address when appropriate (1081 Roosevelt Crescent, North Vancouver, BC V7P 1M4)
- Credentials (licensed, ticketed, insured, registered contractor)
- Specific service areas by name
- Call-to-action at the end

### Things to NEVER Include
- Promises of 24-hour or emergency service (SMR doesn't offer this)
- Service areas outside their coverage (no Surrey, Richmond, Delta)
- Pricing guarantees (use ranges and "get a quote")
- Competitor criticism (recommend partners for services SMR doesn't prioritize)
- Generic stock content without local specificity
